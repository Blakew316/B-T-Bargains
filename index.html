Our Story

From Our Barn to Your Home

B&T Bargains started with a simple idea: good folks shouldn't have to pay big-box prices for everyday goods. So we opened the barn doors in Loudon, Tennessee and started filling the shelves with overstock, closeouts and store returns — all at near half the price.

Every week brings a brand-new haul, so no two visits are ever the same. It's part bargain hunt, part treasure hunt — and it's all about taking care of our neighbors in Loudon County.

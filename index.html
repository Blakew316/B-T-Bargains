About B&T Bargains

A simple idea, run by neighbors.

We started B&T Bargains on a straightforward belief: good people shouldn't have to pay big-box prices for the everyday brands they already use. By buying overstock, closeouts and store returns, we keep quality goods moving and pass the savings on.

New shipments land every week, so the shelves are always changing. It's part errand, part treasure hunt — and it's all about taking care of our neighbors here in Loudon County.

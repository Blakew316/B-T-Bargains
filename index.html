About B&T Bargains

A simple idea, run by neighbors.

We started B&T Bargains on a straightforward belief: good people shouldn't have to pay big-box prices for everyday goods. By buying overstock, closeouts and store returns, we keep quality merchandise moving and pass the savings on.

Every week brings a new shipment, so the shelves are always changing. It's part errand, part treasure hunt — and it's all about taking care of our neighbors here in Loudon County.
